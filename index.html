How to

  • Click photos to select or deselect them.
  • Shift-click to select a range.
  • Use Add to group to bundle selected photos together.
  • Hold the right mouse button on a photo to enlarge it.
  • Double-click a group name to rename it.

Shortcuts

  • 1–9 Add selection to a group
  • Ctrl+A Select this batch
  • Esc Clear selection
